Getting started / 第一次整理

  1. Open folld. Hold Command (⌘) and drag the thin divider to the left of the control button.
    打开 folld，按住 ⌘，将短分隔线拖到控制按钮左侧。
  2. Command-drag optional icons to the left of the divider.
    将不常用的图标拖到分隔线左侧。
  3. Click the control to collapse or expand. Some system icons cannot be moved.
    点击按钮收起或展开。部分系统图标不支持移动。

Right-click the control for Settings. Default shortcut: Control–Option–B; Control–Option–M and Off are also available.
右键按钮打开设置；默认快捷键 ⌃⌥B，可改为 ⌃⌥M 或关闭。

Battery indicator / 低电量提示

When using battery power below your threshold (default 30%, adjustable 10–80%), folld shows its own battery indicator. It hides on external power or at threshold + 3 percentage points. Macs without an internal battery are not supported by this feature.
使用电池且低于阈值时显示 folld 自己的电量指示器；默认30%，可调10–80%。接电或回升至阈值加3个百分点后隐藏。无内置电池的 Mac 不适用。

Wake or display changes expand the group for layout recovery. Report issues with the macOS version, folld version and reproduction steps. Remove private information from screenshots. Never send passwords or verification codes.
唤醒或显示器变化会展开图标以恢复布局。反馈请提供系统与应用版本、重现步骤；截图请遮住私人信息，不要发送密码或验证码。

Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Updated / 更新: 2026-09-20

folld requires no account and includes no ads, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not upload personal information or usage data to developer servers.
folld 不要求账号，不含广告、追踪或第三方分析 SDK，不向开发者服务器上传个人信息或使用数据。

Battery charge, power source, screen sizes and positions of folld's own menu items are processed locally. Preferences such as icon style, threshold and shortcut remain on your Mac. The app does not read the messages, files or menu contents of other apps.
电量、供电状态、屏幕尺寸和 folld 自身菜单栏项目位置仅在本机处理。样式、阈值、快捷键等偏好保存在本机，不读取其他应用的消息、文件或菜单内容。

Apple handles App Store purchases and distribution. folld does not receive payment-card information. Diagnostic sharing depends on your system settings and Apple's policies.
购买与分发由 Apple 处理，folld 不接收付款卡信息。诊断共享取决于系统设置和 Apple 政策。

This static website has no analytics scripts or advertising. GitHub Pages may process access logs for hosting and security: GitHub Privacy Statement.
本站无分析脚本或广告；GitHub Pages 可能为托管与安全处理访问日志。

If you email support, information you voluntarily provide is used to resolve your request. Avoid including unrelated sensitive information. Contact: 183518208@qq.com.
主动邮件反馈的信息仅用于支持处理，请避免提供无关敏感信息。

This policy will be updated if data handling changes.
若数据处理方式改变，本政策将相应更新。
