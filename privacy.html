Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026 · Applies to NotchMate 0.5.0

NotchMate is a local-first macOS utility. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is processed, and where it is stored.

What NotchMate does not do

  • NotchMate does not require an account and does not include advertising, behavioral analytics, or telemetry.
  • NotchMate does not upload your sessions, tasks, token usage, or local files to the developer.
  • NotchMate does not sell personal information or provide cloud sync.

Data accessed locally

After you explicitly authorize a folder with the macOS system picker, the App Store build reads the selected .codex data directory in read-only mode. On your Mac, NotchMate may read provider availability, task and session state, titles, turn counts, token and context usage, elapsed time, quota windows, and local usage records.

SmartWork data is read from a local service on your computer at 127.0.0.1. NotchMate does not forward data from that local service to the developer.

Network access

To measure OpenAI service-path reachability and latency, NotchMate sends a credential-free HEAD request to https://api.openai.com/v1/models. The request does not contain NotchMate account credentials, Codex session content, or local file content. The destination service and network providers may process IP addresses, request times, and ordinary network metadata under their own policies. The NotchMate developer does not receive this probe data.

Apple processes App Store downloads, purchases, updates, and related transactions under Apple’s privacy policy.

Data stored locally

NotchMate may store the following in its macOS App Sandbox container:

  • Settings such as display mode, refresh profile, status companion, and provider display names.
  • A security-scoped bookmark for the .codex folder you authorize.
  • A local daily usage index that reduces repeated scanning.

This information remains on your Mac.

Retention, deletion, and access removal

Local settings and indexes remain until you delete NotchMate’s App Sandbox container data. You can quit or uninstall NotchMate and disable it in macOS login items. The developer cannot remotely access or delete this local data for you.

Children and policy changes

NotchMate is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information. If the app’s data practices change, this page will show a new effective date and, when appropriate, the change will also be disclosed on the product page or in the app.
